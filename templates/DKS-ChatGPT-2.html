DKS checkpoint slider

Click a checkpoint to set its value to 1. Drag right from that checkpoint to extend its active span. The line stops immediately before the destination checkpoint.

Gesture: press directly on a checkpoint. Release without moving = value 1. Drag right = 2 / 3 / 4 where allowed. Use × below a checkpoint to return it to 0. Keyboard: focus a checkpoint, use ← / →, and Delete to disable.
